Frequently asked questions

How do I transfer files from PC to phone without internet?

Open Send on your computer and choose a file — the screen turns it into a stream of animated QR codes. Open Receive on your phone, point the camera at the screen, and the file rebuilds itself. No internet, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth is used between the devices; the data travels as light.

Does it work without Wi-Fi or Bluetooth?

Yes. There is no network path between the two devices. The sender only shows QR codes and the receiver only reads them with its camera — no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cable or pairing required.

Is it private and safe?

The file never leaves the room. Nothing is uploaded to a server or the cloud, so it's ideal for air-gapped or sensitive machines.

How fast is it and how big can files be?

It runs at roughly 100 KB/s — best for documents, photos, keys, configs and small archives up to a few megabytes. Larger files work but take longer. The file is verified by hash on arrival, so it's either correct or it keeps going.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, on both the computer and the phone.
